Privacy Policy

Test version · Effective November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Mira mobile application (the “App”), developed as part of the InclusiveTech project, handles information. The App helps deaf, hard-of-hearing and hearing people communicate by recognizing sign language and converting speech to text.

1. Data controller and contact

The data controller is Ekaterina Sergeevna Rassuditelnova (Рассудительнова Екатерина Сергеевна). For privacy questions and deletion requests, contact khurtovaekaterina@gmail.com.

2. Video and recognition history

When sign-language recognition is used, the App accesses camera video and sends it to a server to recognize signs and convert them to text. Video recordings are also retained so that users can view their recording and recognition history.

Videos are retained for no longer than 7 calendar days after the server receives them. They are automatically deleted at the end of that period. No backup copies of video recordings are created.

Videos may show the user or other people in the frame. In the test version, videos are not used to train AI models, for advertising, or to create advertising profiles.

3. Speech and microphone

The App may access the microphone to convert spoken language to text. In the current test version, speech recognition is performed on the device. Audio recordings are not sent to the server or stored by the data controller.

4. Device technical information

For technical compatibility and App operation, the App uses and sends limited device information to the server: device manufacturer and model, Android version, and technical characteristics needed for its functions.

The test version does not use a separate analytics system or intentionally collect crash reports. During network communications, the server infrastructure may technically process connection information, including IP addresses, as needed to receive and service requests. This information is not used for advertising profiling.

5. Accounts and correspondence

The current test version does not require users to create an account or provide a name, telephone number, or email address to use the App. If a user contacts the data controller by email, the contact details in that message are processed to reply and handle the request.

6. Purposes and user notice

Data is used to recognize sign language, provide recognition history for a limited period, deliver App functionality, and maintain technical operation.

Before the first video upload, the App informs users that video is sent to a server and stored for up to 7 days, links to this Policy, and offers a way to decline the upload. Where consent is required, it is obtained through a separate affirmative action. If the purposes or conditions of processing materially change, updated information is provided and renewed consent is obtained where necessary.

7. Hosting, access, and security

Video processing and storage use the App’s server infrastructure located in the Russian Federation. Video is transmitted to the server over HTTPS and is kept in encrypted storage. Access to stored recordings is restricted to authorized developers who need it to operate the App.

The App may use technical infrastructure providers that process data on the operator’s behalf to the extent necessary to deliver their services. The test version does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. Data is not provided to third parties for their own advertising purposes. Information about processors is provided where and as required by applicable law.

8. Deletion and user rights

Video is automatically deleted no later than 7 calendar days after reaching the server. Users may request earlier deletion of a specific recording by writing to khurtovaekaterina@gmail.com. An authorized developer reviews the request and manually deletes the recording after identifying the relevant file and verifying the request.

Users may also contact this address to request information about processing, correction, restriction or deletion of their data, or to withdraw consent. Requests are handled according to applicable law and its time limits. Information needed to handle a request is used only for that purpose and retained as legally required.

9. Policy updates

This Policy will be updated if the App's functionality or data practices change. The current version is available at https://mira.inclusivetech.ru/privacy/.
